# Official Source Register

This register is a starting point, not an exhaustive or permanently current legal inventory. Verify the current version before relying on a requirement.

## RBI — Fraud Risk Management Directions for Commercial Banks and AIFIs
- Official text: https://systemhealth.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx_id%3D12702%281%29.html
- RBI press release: https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=58294
- Direction reference: RBI/DOS/2024-25/118, DOS.CO.FMG.SEC.No.5/23.04.001/2024-25, dated 15 July 2024.
- Relevant concepts: governance, EWS, RFA, investigation, reporting and closure.
- Caution: confirm applicability and latest amendments before treating a provision as a requirement for a particular entity.

## RBI — Fraud Risk Management Directions for Cooperative Banks

- Caution: separate direction and scope from commercial banks/AIFIs.

## RBI — Fraud Risk Management Directions for NBFCs
- Official source: https://systemhealth.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx_id%3D12704.html

## RBI Annual Report 2024–25
- Official source: https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/AnnualReportPublications.aspx?Id=1436
- Relevant context: fraud reporting by category, including card/internet and advances/loan portfolio; amount involved is not necessarily amount diverted.

## Ministry of Home Affairs — I4C Suspect Registry parliamentary response
- Official PDF: https://www.mha.gov.in/MHA1/Par2017/pdfs/par2026-pdfs/LS17032026/3913.pdf
- Dated: 17 March 2026.
- Relevant context: I4C Suspect Registry and reported Layer 1 mule-account/declined-transaction figures as of 31 January 2026.
- Caution: registry/activity statistics are not proof of guilt or realized loss prevented.

## Payment rails
For UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, card, and cross-border payment details, consult current official RBI/NPCI/payment-network documentation for the specific rail. Do not encode current limits, service hours, settlement rules, dispute windows, or fees from memory. Add a dated source entry here before relying on them.

## Source maintenance
When adding a regulatory claim, record:
- claim and relevant entity/product scope,
- exact official source and section,
- publication/effective/update date,
- date last checked,
- implementation impact,
- open questions and reviewer.
